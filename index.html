FROM MANUSCRIPT TO BOOK

Your words.
Ready to publish.

Book4matter turns Word, plain text, and Markdown manuscripts into formatted books, handling typography, chapter layout, and page numbering. Add your chapters and create a book for print, screens, or an e-reader. Everything happens in your browser. None of your files are uploaded to a server.

BUILD YOUR BOOK

Start with your chapters.

Add Word (.docx), Markdown (.md), or plain text (.txt) files, in as many batches as you like. Add images, fonts, book_metadata.yaml, or book_style.yaml if you have them. If your files include or reference files in other directories, we recommend adding the whole book folder so relative paths still work. No settings files?
